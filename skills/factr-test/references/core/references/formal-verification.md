# Formal conclusion profile

Read only for formal compliance, certification, baseline acceptance, or definitive verification. This adds decision criteria to the same behavioral methodology; it does not establish a sequence of approved intermediate artifacts.

Determine the requested decision, applicable obligation set, source authority and revision, scope, evidence, and required sign-off from supplied material and applicable policy. Informal notes remain useful context but do not silently become authoritative obligations. A repository implementation or passing test cannot supply its own missing acceptance criterion.

Allocate assertions to the narrowest supported obligations. Distinguish direct support, inference, proposed extensions, conflicts, uncovered obligations, and exclusions. Resolve references before asserting complete coverage. A partial source review supports a scoped mapping, not an exhaustive assessment.

Evaluate actual evidence for the right target and revision, relevant conditions, observation quality, and coverage of the claimed conclusion. An observation of configuration acceptance does not establish effective application; one observation surface cannot stand in for another without a justified relationship. Missing, stale, uncorrelated, or insufficient evidence limits the verdict.

Report separately:

- What the criteria require, with source/version and reviewed scope.
- Which assertions and actual observations address those criteria.
- What the observations support or contradict within their scope.
- What remains inconclusive, conflicted, untested, or outside the review.
- Whether required formal acceptance or sign-off exists, when applicable.

Do not fabricate reviewer acceptance or treat elapsed time, silence, a generated export, or a structural validator pass as approval. Preserve exact accepted source/baseline revisions where policy requires them. A changed working assertion becomes a linked revision; it does not silently amend the accepted baseline. Identify affected conclusions and apply applicable re-review rules.

If a definitive decision lacks support, state the precise missing criterion, evidence, or acceptance decision and why it matters. Deliver the available allocation, candidate tests, and evidence-gap analysis. Ask for the smallest material missing item when needed; do not repeatedly request sources already supplied. Neither the missing formal decision nor this profile prevents exploratory assertions or proposed automation.
